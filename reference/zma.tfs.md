# Maize transcription factors

Transcription factors and their families were downloaded from PlantTFDB
4.0.

## Usage

``` r
data(zma.tfs)
```

## Format

A data frame with gene IDs of TFs and their associated families.

## References

Jin, J., Tian, F., Yang, D. C., Meng, Y. Q., Kong, L., Luo, J., & Gao,
G. (2016). PlantTFDB 4.0: toward a central hub for transcription factors
and regulatory interactions in plants. Nucleic acids research, gkw982.

## Examples

``` r
data(zma.tfs)
```
